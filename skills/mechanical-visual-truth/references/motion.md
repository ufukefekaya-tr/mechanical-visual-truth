# Motion and animation

Two modes have different evidentiary weight.

## Deterministic CAD

Keyframes transform named entities from the same B-rep registry used for STEP and proof PNG. Sample coupled actors, declared collision pairs, clearances, counts, and camera at every audit sample. Output MP4, reduced preview GIF, six-frame contact sheet, and `animation-manifest.json`.

## Presentation-only 2.5D

Use hash-verified accepted backgrounds and isolated actor sprites. Maintain a fixed camera, identical transforms for mechanically coupled actors, smooth time interpolation, explicit process arrows, and a burned-in concept disclaimer. This technique is useful when full CAD kinematics are unavailable, but it is not physics, collision, or manufacturing proof.

Historical regression: a carriage moved while its payload remained static. Always test coupled motion as a relative-transform invariant.
