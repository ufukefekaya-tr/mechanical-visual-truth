# Promotion policy

Scores total 100: severity 25, evidence quality 25, reproducibility 20, independent recurrence 15, generality 10, regression strength 5.

Automatic eligibility requires score >=85, evidence, a regression that failed before and passes after, no baseline regression, no duplicate or conflicting rule, a clean secret scan, and rollback material.

- 85+: Shield and relevant skill reference
- 92+: repo AGENTS when at least two independent events or one critical deterministic failure exists
- 95+: repo-local generated system prompt when at least three independent events or one critical deterministic failure exists

User-specific aesthetic and camera preferences remain in the project contract. During five successful runs the rule is `SOAKING`. A critical failure triggers automatic rollback and quarantine.
